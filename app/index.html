P1 Verified Dispatch Token — Biometric + Geolocation Binding

This is the technical mechanism, not just "we send an OTP" — a single cryptographic token binds the attendant's biometric confirmation, their live GPS coordinate, and the timestamp together at the moment of check-in, so the record cannot be replayed, reused, or falsified after the fact.

P2 Autonomous Sensor-Triggered Escalation — Simulated Wearable

A live vitals stream (heart rate, SpO2) feeds directly into CHAKRA's existing dispatch/escalation engine. No human has to notice something is wrong and press a button — the system detects the threshold breach itself and fires the same RED-tier escalation used elsewhere in this app, autonomously.
